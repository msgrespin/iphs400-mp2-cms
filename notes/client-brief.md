# Client Brief - AWM at Kenyon

## Who runs the site
The site is run by the officers the organization, the 2 co-VPs maintain the site. 

## What Positions have control over the site
Co-VPS are admin. Co-Presidents, Social Chair, and Treasurer are editors. Editors publish and delete posts, they will be able to edit pages but never delete them
The positions change so each new person will have different levels of computing expertise.

## Who is the site for
The site is for members and curious students. Information on the site is about when and where Study Hall is and how to join. 
We also have a link to a google form for snack requests.

## Issue with the site right now
The old site means editing raw HTML. THe time and place is in 3 files. The weekly email is currently our only form of communication.

## What would make this better
A new officer can post the Monday Study Hall info without even learning HTML

## Forms of communication
The weekly emails stay manual, with a link to the website post

## Extra Notes
- Meetings are called Study Halls.
- An edior is never allowed to delete a page of the website.
