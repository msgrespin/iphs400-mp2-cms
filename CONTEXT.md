# AWM at Kenyon CMS

The website system AWM's Officers use to tell members and curious students when
and where Study Hall is and how to join.

## Language

### People

**Officer**:
A student who currently holds a Position in AWM.

**Position**:
A named Officer job: Co-VP, Co-President, Social Chair, or Treasurer.
_Avoid_: title, job

**Account**:
The login for a Position, used by whoever holds that Position. It belongs to the Position, not to a person, so the two Co-VPs share one Account and the two Co-Presidents share another.
_Avoid_: user, personal login

**Role**:
What an Account is allowed to do. There are two: Admin and Editor.

**Admin**:
The Role of the Co-VPs, who maintain the site, create and delete Pages, manage Accounts, and Go live.

**Editor**:
The Role of the Co-Presidents, Social Chair, and Treasurer, who write, publish, and delete Posts and edit the text of existing Pages. An Editor never creates or deletes a Page.

**Deactivated**:
The state of an Account whose Position is vacant. It cannot sign in, its Posts stay on the site, and it can be reactivated.
_Avoid_: deleted, removed

**Handover**:
The yearly passing of Account passwords from the spring semester's Officers to the fall semester's.

**Visitor**:
A member or curious student reading the public site. A Visitor never logs in.

### Content

**Study Hall**:
AWM's weekly meeting.
_Avoid_: meeting

**Post**:
A dated news item on the site. It is the only kind of dated content.
_Avoid_: announcement, update

**Study Hall reminder**:
The weekly Monday Post about that night's Study Hall. Nearly every Post is one.

**Author**:
The Position whose Account wrote a Post. Officers see it; Visitors do not.

**Weekly email**:
The message Officers send by hand each week. Its text is the same as that week's Study Hall reminder, plus a link to the Post.

**Page**:
A permanent part of the site's structure. There are three: Home, About, and Join & Snacks.

**Home**:
The Page that holds the standing Study Hall time and place and shows the latest Published Post beneath it. Home always exists and is always Published.

**Past posts**:
The list of older Published Posts that Visitors reach from Home. It is not a Page and no Officer creates it.

### Publishing

**Draft**:
A Post or Page still being written, which Visitors cannot see.

**Published**:
The status an Officer gives a Post or Page when it is ready for Visitors. Published content reaches Visitors only at the next Go live.

**Go live**:
The step, done by the Co-VPs, that puts everything Published onto the public site.
_Avoid_: deploy, export

**Not live yet**:
A change made since the last Go live, which Visitors therefore cannot see.

**Link**:
The web address of a Post, made from its date and title. It stops changing once the Post is Published, so a Link sent in a Weekly email keeps working.
_Avoid_: slug, permalink (in anything an Officer sees)
